# Layer "plates": summary

- **What it is:** seven present-day plates (Westeros, Essos, Sothoryos, Ulthos, the oceanic Sunset plate, the
  southern continent, the south polar plate) with ridges, trenches and transforms, and the land frame of the planet:
  canon land plus frames of the lands outside the map, 28.8 % land in all. Generation: `GENERATION.md`.
- **Interpretation (E05):** the narrow sea is a young opening ocean (like the Red Sea); the Summer Sea closes as
  Sothoryos moves north (like the Mediterranean and Africa), with the Valyrian volcanic arc above its trench (like the
  Aegean arc); Ulthos meets eastern Essos across the Jade Sea arcs (like Australia and Indonesia); the Bone Mountains
  are an old reactivated suture (Urals, Tian Shan); western Westeros is a passive margin. Agreed by the project owner.
- **Design (E06):** frames placed and sized to the agreed targets; places lie on their designed plates; no continent
  is cut by a ridge.
- **Next:** the geological history (stage 3), which ends in this state and shapes the unknown lands.
