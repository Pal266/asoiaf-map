# Layer "canon relief": summary

- **What it is:** 34 canon relief features of the known world (mountain ranges, hills, volcanoes, deserts, a canyon,
  inland lakes, the Neck, the Stepstones) as envelopes and points in longitude/latitude and on the global grid, with
  the density of relief drawn on the official map inside mountain and hill envelopes. Generation: `GENERATION.md`.
- **Use:** targets the geological history must explain (stage 1).
- **Shares of the known land:** mountains 16 %, hills 7 %, deserts 8 %, volcanic 2 %.
- **Experiment:** E04 (automatic detection of relief failed against the map's other drawings; envelopes drawn by eye,
  with the drawn relief measured inside them).
