# Source maps

The maps are copyrighted and are **not** kept in this repository. Each entry says how to recognise the file the
scripts expect.

| Id | Map | Grade | File | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | "The Known World", *The Lands of Ice and Fire* (2012), © George R. R. Martin; drawn by Jonathan Roberts | A (official) | scan, 9050 × 6000 px, JPEG, 28 766 695 bytes | outlines of the continents and islands; relief, forests and deserts (later layers) |
| M2 | "World of Ice and Fire", fan map of the known world with a scale bar in miles | B (derived from the official maps) | 1024 × 682 px, PNG | cross-check of the scale only |

Grades as in `world/canon/CONSTRAINTS.md`.

## Notes

- M1 has no scale. Its scale comes from the Wall (100 leagues, ~480 km, coast to coast): see experiment E01.
- On M2 the Wall measures ~305 miles against its scale bar, which agrees with the canon 300 miles.
