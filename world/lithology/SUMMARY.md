# Lithology — summary

- **What it is:** the surface rock of every 10 km cell in the 16 classes of the Global Lithological Map (GLiM),
  plus the surface deposits (alluvium, evaporite, aeolian sand, loess) and the bedrock under them.
- **The model:**
  - each tectonic province of the geological history has a rock composition from textbook geology;
  - smooth random fields turn the shares into coherent outcrops, and warp the province contacts;
  - cratons split into exposed shield and platform cover by relief;
  - deposits come from the hydrology and the climate.
- **Check against Earth:** the land shares of the main groups are within 1–5 points of GLiM.
- **The world:**
  - the North has shield gneiss and granites;
  - the south of Westeros has platform sandstones and limestones, loess plains and Dornish sand;
  - the Dothraki Sea is a loess belt;
  - Valyria has andesites and pyroclastics.
- **Feeds:** the soils' parent material (Arenosols on sand, Vertisols on basalt and clay plains, Andosols on young
  volcanics, Nitisols on tropical basalts, Leptosols on limestone, Podzols and Luvisols on sand and loess).
