# Resources (ore bodies and area resources) — summary

- **What it is:**
  - about 70 000 mineral deposits of 21 types, each with its place, ore tonnage, grades and contained metal (gold,
    silver, copper, iron, tin, lead, zinc, nickel, mercury);
  - maps of area resources: coal, salt, bog iron, peat, clay, building stones, obsidian, sulfur.
- **The model:** the USGS three-part assessment, run forward:
  - tracts where the geology permits each deposit type (province, rock, age, intrusions, faults, climate);
  - the number of deposits from Singer's deposit-density regression;
  - sizes and grades from the USGS grade-and-tonnage models.
- **Check against Earth:**
  - copper, nickel and deposit counts are within a factor of 2–3 of Earth;
  - lead and mercury are 3–4 times higher;
  - zinc is about 10 times too high (MVT deposits), and tin is probably too high.
- **Canon:**
  - the Westerlands are a gold belt, with the richest gold and silver of Westeros (decision 13);
  - the Iron Islands have iron, lead and tin with little gold (decision 12);
  - Valyria's Fourteen Flames hold gold, silver, copper, tin and iron;
  - Dragonstone has obsidian.
