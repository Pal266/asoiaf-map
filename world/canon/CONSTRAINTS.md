# Canon constraints for the world map

What canon says about the geography of the known world. These are the targets the geological history and the later
layers must reproduce or, where science does not allow it, depart from with an entry in `CANON_DEVIATIONS.md`
(Rule 2).

## Reliability

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Stated in the novels, *The World of Ice and Fire*, *Fire & Blood*, or drawn on the official maps (*The Lands of Ice and Fire*). |
| **B** | Stated by George R. R. Martin outside the books (interviews, letters): semi-canon. |
| **C** | In-world uncertainty: legend, rumour, or a maester's guess. A target only where science agrees. |

Facts were found through [A Wiki of Ice and Fire](https://awoiaf.westeros.org/) (AWOIAF), which cites the book for
each; the source column names the AWOIAF page.

## 6. Conflicts within canon

1. **The size of Westeros.** "Similar in size to South America" (WE3) does not match the Wall scale (WE2): South
   America is ~7 500 km north to south (12° N to 56° S) and ~17.8 million km² in area, while the Wall scale gives ~4 800 km and an area
   nearer to Europe's. The Wall scale (100 leagues) comes from a stated measurement; the comparison is a loose remark.
   Resolved: the Wall scale (section 7).
2. **The size of the planet.** Martin suggests the planet may be a little larger than Earth (W2); the project uses
   Earth's size (`world/planet/PLANET.md`). The statement is a guess, not a fact, so it is noted here, not logged as a
   deviation.
3. **Magic as a cause.** The Arm of Dorne (SE3) and the Doom of Valyria (E10) are explained by magic in some in-world
   accounts. Both have natural explanations in canon itself (sinking land and rising sea; a volcanic eruption) and the
   model uses those.

## 7. Project owner's decisions

1. **Scale: the Wall.** Westeros is scaled by the Wall, 100 leagues (~480 km), which gives ~4 800 km north to south
   and ~1 450 km at its widest (WE2). The comparison with South America (WE3) is not followed: see
   `CANON_DEVIATIONS.md`.
2. **Outlines as in canon.** The outlines of the continents stay as on the canon maps wherever science allows. Where
   it does not, the change is logged in `CANON_DEVIATIONS.md`.
3. **The map lies on the globe as a sinusoidal projection centred on the Wall** (experiment
   `world/canon_outline/experiments/E02_projection.md`): the map's rows are parallels; distances along every parallel
   and along the Wall's meridian, and all areas, are as drawn; the Wall's midpoint is at **63.75° N**, the middle of
   the range (63.5–64° N) in which every canon climate region falls in its Earth latitude band.

4. **Land covers ~29 % of the planet, as on Earth** (~148 million km²). The planet has Earth's parameters, and the
   climate, ocean and relief models are calibrated on Earth, where land and ocean are shared in this way. The known
   world gives ~32 million km² (~6 %).
5. **The Sunset Sea is an ocean the size of the Pacific**, about 180° of longitude (~15 000 km at mid-latitudes), with
   islands at most and no continent on the routes west from Westeros. This follows what most in canon believe and
   explains why no one has crossed it.
6. **Target areas of the land outside the map** (frames for the geological history, which sets the actual shapes):

   | Land | Area, approx. | Basis |
   |---|---|---|
   | Rest of Essos, east of the map | ~20 M km² | only ~24 M km² of room lies between the map's east edge and an east coast ~180° from Westeros (decision 5); Essos ~41 M km² in all, the size of Asia (deviation 4) |
   | Rest of Sothoryos, to the south | ~31 M km² | "perhaps as long as Essos is wide" (S2); analogue Africa (~32 M km² with the part on the map) |
   | Ulthos | ~10 M km² | a large landmass of unknown size (U1) |
   | South polar continent | ~14 M km² | analogue Antarctica |
   | Unknown continent in the south of the Sunset Sea | ~39 M km² | Martin is not sure whether counterparts of the Americas exist (W4); in the south it lies off the routes from Westeros |

   The whole southern hemisphere lies outside the map (the map covers 6–74° N). Revised by the project owner on
   2026-11-18: the first draft gave the rest of Essos ~34 M km², which does not fit with decision 5; the ~14 M km²
   difference moved to Sothoryos and the southern continent.
7. **The northern Sunset Sea has a warm drift like the North Atlantic's** (experiment
   `world/climate/experiments/E09_temperature.md`). First decided the other way on 2026-11-21 (no warm current,
   Pacific-type). The same day the model showed that without it the North of Westeros would be like Labrador, with
   the Haunted Forest as tundra, so the decision was reversed. The drift is a prescribed ocean heat flux calibrated
   on the North Atlantic and placed on the eastern side of the northern Sunset Sea.
8. **No ice sheet in the north; an ice sheet on the south polar continent** (experiment
   `world/ice/experiments/E11_ice.md`, decided 2026-11-22). The world lies at the threshold of glaciation, and its
   northern ice is bistable. Either the Land of Always Winter's sheet spreads to the Wall and beyond, or there is
   none. The owner first chose canon within the model's uncertainty (daily temperature spread σ 5 K), which gave a
   sheet reaching the Wall. Then, after the bistability showed, he chose σ 5.5 K and no northern sheet (canon
   deviation 6). The south polar continent starts glaciated, because the geological history has kept it at the
   pole, like Antarctica.
9. **The rivers of the official map are carved into the relief** (experiment
   `world/hydrology/experiments/E12_hydrology.md`, decided 2026-11-22). The relief (E08) has no river incision, and
   its drainage did not follow the canon rivers (the Riverlands drained west, the Rhoyne barely reached Volantis).
   The main rivers are traced on M1 and carved as valleys falling from source to mouth. Their discharge comes from
   the climate.
10. **The inland lakes of the official map hold water** (experiment E12, decided 2026-11-22): the God's Eye, Dagger
    Lake, the Shrinking Sea (its two remaining pools) and the Bleeding Sea are taken from M1. Their water balance
    decides whether they overflow; the Shrinking Sea and the Bleeding Sea, in a dry interior, are terminal lakes
    losing more by evaporation than they receive.
11. **The climate of the North is kept; its vegetation follows the plants' physiology** (experiments
    `world/vegetation/experiments/E15_vegetation.md` and E09, addendum 2; decided 2026-11-22). With the forest
    limit of the BIOME models the North of Westeros is forest-tundra and bog with taiga in its south, and the Haunted
    Forest is tundra. The owner had the climate of the North re-examined first: the model shows no cold error at
    Earth's maritime subarctic stations, and the cool summers come from the cold seas around the North. He chose
    the canon deviation (deviation 5, extended) over moving the map south.
12. **A Variscan terrane under the Iron Islands** (experiment `world/resources/experiments/E17_resources.md`,
    decided 2026-11-22). In the geological history the islands were young platform limestone, which can hold lead
    but neither iron nor tin (WE12). The Variscan arc terrane of the Westerlands hills (300 Ma) now continues north
    under Ironman's Bay, as Cornwall's granites continue into the Scilly Isles. Its granites, skarns and veins carry
    tin, lead and iron. In the lithology the terrane has its own composition (granite and diorite bosses in slate,
    limestone and greenstone, as in Cornwall), drawn at a ~30 km outcrop scale with the intrusions next to the
    limestone, so that the small islands have the contacts that make skarns.
13. **The Westerlands hills are a gold belt** (experiment E17, decided 2026-11-22). Treated like every other old fold
    belt, the Westerlands held 5.7 % of Westeros's gold on 9.3 % of its area, against canon (WE11). The hills are
    now an accreted Variscan island-arc terrane: turbidites, greenstones, metavolcanics and granitoids, cut by a
    dense network of shear zones, like the Lachlan orogen of Victoria. They then hold 23 % of Westeros's gold and
    56 % of its silver.

## 8. Not stated by canon

The shapes of the coasts outside the official maps, the far north of Westeros, the south of Sothoryos, the whole of
Ulthos, the lands beyond the Sunset Sea and the rest of the planet. These are filled in by the geological history,
grounded in science (Rule 1).
