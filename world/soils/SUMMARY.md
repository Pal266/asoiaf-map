# Soils — summary

- **What it is:** the dominant soil of every 10 km cell, as a WRB Reference Soil Group, with a natural fertility index
  (0–100) for the game.
- **The model:** a key following the soil-forming factors in the FAO order. It goes through:
  - water, ice, peat and salt;
  - permafrost;
  - relief, parent rock (from the lithology layer, E14) and floodplains;
  - deserts, tropics, steppes, the Mediterranean and the temperate and boreal zones, by Thornthwaite's moisture
    classes.
- **Earth test:** areas of the groups within a factor ~2 of the FAO estimates. Half of 46 textbook regions are
  right. Most misses come from the climate model's errors on Earth, not from the key.
- **The world:**
  - the North has podzols and peat, with permafrost beyond the Wall;
  - the Riverlands have Luvisols and Cambisols, and the Neck is gley marsh;
  - the dry Reach and Crownlands have Kastanozems and Calcisols (canon deviation 7);
  - Dorne has sands and calcareous desert soils, the loess plains carry Kastanozems and Luvisols;
  - Valyria has volcanic Andosols, and the great rivers are lined with Fluvisols.
