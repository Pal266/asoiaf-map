# Temperature — summary

- **What it is:** monthly mean temperature of every 10 km cell (ground level on land, sea surface at sea), from a
  physical model.
- **The model:** a two-layer seasonal energy balance model on the sphere. It has:
  - Earth's orbit, separate surface and air layers, winds, continentality and sea ice;
  - a warm drift in the north-east Sunset Sea (decision 7);
  - snow on high ground and the ice sheets of the ice layer (E11).

  It was calibrated and tested on 78 Earth stations: errors of 3–5 °C, with subarctic summers within 1–3 °C.
- **The world** (with the ice; global mean 13.4 °C):

  | Place | January | July |
  |---|---|---|
  | Winterfell | −11 °C | 11 °C |
  | King's Landing | 3 °C | 19 °C |
  | Sunspear | 15 °C | 25 °C |
  | Qarth | 18 °C | 29 °C |

  North of the Wall the summers are too cool for dense forest: the Haunted Forest is sparse taiga at the edge of the
  forest-tundra (canon deviation 5).
