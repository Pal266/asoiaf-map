# Hydrology — summary

- **What it is:** for every 10 km cell, annual runoff, river discharge and flow direction, and lakes, marshes and
  salt flats. Built from the climate with the ice and from the relief.
- **The model:**
  - runoff from the Budyko water balance;
  - flow routed over the land surface;
  - lakes in depressions: they overflow, or end their rivers where evaporation wins.
- **Canon:** the main rivers and lakes of the official map are traced on it and built into the routing (decisions
  9 and 10). The Trident, the Blackwater Rush, the White Knife, the Mander and the Rhoyne with its tributaries flow
  along their drawn courses.
- **Earth test:**
  - global runoff to the sea as on Earth;
  - river discharges are within a factor ~2–3, limited by the precipitation model;
  - where the precipitation is right (Lena, Mackenzie, Yangtze) they are within 10–60 %.
- **The world:**

  | River | Discharge |
  |---|---|
  | White Knife | ~4 000 m³/s |
  | Trident | ~3 100 m³/s |
  | Rhoyne at Volantis | ~1 200 m³/s, from 1.4 M km² of western Essos |
  | Mander | ~240 m³/s |
  | Blackwater Rush | ~100 m³/s |

  - The dry Mediterranean south of Westeros keeps its rivers small (canon deviation 7).
  - The Neck is marsh.
  - The Bleeding Sea and the Shrinking Sea are terminal salt lakes.
