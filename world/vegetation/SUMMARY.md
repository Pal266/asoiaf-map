# Vegetation — summary

- **What it is:** the potential natural vegetation of every 10 km cell (the landscape before people), as one of
  21 biomes, with its net primary productivity.
- **The model:** a bioclimatic key in the manner of the BIOME models:
  - winter cold, summer heat and a moisture index (actual over potential evapotranspiration) admit plant types;
  - the soil layer's peat and the hydrology's marshes make wetlands and swamp forests;
  - large rivers line dry land with riparian woodland;
  - productivity comes from the Miami model.
- **Earth test:** on the model's own climate of Earth, the biome areas are within a factor ~1.3 of the WWF map for
  most biomes, and 64 % of 78 textbook sites are right. Misses follow the climate model's errors on Earth.
- **The forest limit** is the physiological one of BIOME1 (350 degree-days above 5 °C). On the model's Earth climate
  it cannot be calibrated: the arctic summers err both ways.
- **The world:**
  - the North is forest-tundra and bog, with taiga in its south (canon deviation 5, extended after a re-examination
    of the climate);
  - the Haunted Forest and the land beyond the Wall are tundra;
  - closed forest begins at the Neck; the Riverlands and the Vale are broadleaf forest;
  - the Reach and the Crownlands are Mediterranean woodland, and Dorne is xeric scrub and desert;
  - the Dothraki sea is grassland and the Red Waste desert.
