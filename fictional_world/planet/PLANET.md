# Planet parameters

**Decision (project owner, 2026-11-22):** the planet has Earth's parameters: Earth's size, year, axial tilt and distance from the
Sun.

**Scientific justification (Rule 1):** these are measured values of a real planet that supports the climates, rivers
and life the world needs. Every model of the later layers (climate, hydrology, vegetation) can be calibrated directly
on Earth data.

## Values

All values are Earth's, from the [NASA Earth Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/earthfact.html).

## Moon

**Decision (project owner, 2026-11-22, with "a planet like Earth"):** the planet has one moon with the parameters of Earth's Moon. Its main effect on the map
is the tides, which shape coasts, estuaries and deltas.

Values from the [NASA Moon Fact Sheet](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/moonfact.html).

| Parameter | Value |

## Consequences

- One year of four regular seasons, as on Earth.
- Day length, insolation, tides and the climate models of the later layers can be checked directly against Earth data.
